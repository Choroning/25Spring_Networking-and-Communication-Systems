## 📝 About This Repository

- `Lecture-Notes/` holds lecture notes by week (`Week##`), and the several lecture decks and appendices covered in one week are organized together in that week's single lecture note.
- `Coursework/` holds the three assignment handouts and the assignment explanations.
- Every lecture note and assignment explanation comes as an English (`.md`) and Korean (`.ko.md`) pair.

## 📚 Course Information

- **Semester:** Spring 2025 (March - June)
- **Affiliation:** Korea University Sejong

| Course&nbsp;Code| Course            | Type          | Instructor      | Department                              |
|:----------:|:------------------|:-------------:|:---------------:|:----------------------------------------|
|`DAMO423-00`|NETWORKING AND COMMUNICATION SYSTEMS|Major Elective|Prof. Si-bok&nbsp;Yu|Department of Autonomous Mobility|

### Course Overview

A major elective course on the wired in-vehicle communication of autonomous vehicles (CAN, LIN, FlexRay, automotive Ethernet) and V2X wireless communication linked with C-ITS (DSRC, WAVE, C-V2X, CALM), starting from the basics of data communication. The instructor, schedule, assessment, course policies, and progress are in the [course information document](Course-Information.md).

<br><a name="prerequisites"></a>
## ✅ Prerequisites

- No prerequisite course is designated.
- The course covers the basics of data communication and networking so that students without background in communication can take it.
