## Question 1. ASK, FSK, and PSK (10 points)

Explain the characteristics of each of the amplitude modulation method (amplitude-shift keying; ASK), the frequency modulation method (frequency-shift keying; FSK), and the phase modulation method (phase-shift keying; PSK). (10 points)

---

<br>

## Question 2. Sampling Rate (10 points)

When sampling a signal to encode an analog signal into digital form, explain why the sampling rate must be at least twice the frequency of the analog signal. Include a figure and an explanation. (10 points)

---

<br>

## Question 3. FDM and TDM (10 points)

Explain the characteristics of frequency-division multiplexing (FDM) and time-division multiplexing (TDM), and present their differences. (10 points)

---

<br>

## Question 4. The Five TCP/IP Layers and the Seven OSI Layers (10 points)

List the five layers of TCP/IP and explain the characteristics of each. Describe where each of the five layers falls in comparison with the OSI 7 layers. (10 points)

---

<br>

## Question 5. The IP Header (10 points)

Explain why the IP header is added in an IP datagram and what its role is. (10 points)

<br>

## Question 6. The Network Layer and the Transport Layer (10 points)

Among the OSI 7 layers, explain the differences in the characteristics and roles of the network layer and the transport layer. (10 points)
