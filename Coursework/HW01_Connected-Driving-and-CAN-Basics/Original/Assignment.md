## Question 1. Toward Connected Automated Driving (10 points)

**1.1** Explain three or more reasons why autonomous vehicles need to evolve into connected automated driving with V2X communication. (5 points)

**1.2** Then explain the uses of connected automated driving with safety and convenience in mind. (5 points)

---

<br>

## Question 2. Use of Automotive Ethernet (10 points)

**2.1** In the network architecture of an autonomous vehicle, list two module components connected to Automotive Ethernet. (5 points)

Core module components of an autonomous vehicle: among RADAR, LIDAR, Camera, HVI, ADR, DCU, the Map module, the Positioning module, the V2X OBU, the BIS display, the ESU, and others, which modules use Automotive Ethernet?

**2.2** Explain why these components use Automotive Ethernet. (5 points)

<br>

## Question 3. Understanding the Autonomous Driving Levels (10 points)

**3.1** Derive the key keywords that separate the stages of the autonomous driving levels (SAE levels = ISO levels). (5 points)

**3.2** Based on these, explain the differences between the levels. (5 points)
